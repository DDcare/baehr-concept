## What we found

We looked at the site from the outside, as any visitor would, without logging in and without your figures.

The foundations are good: BAEHR España has a very clear niche, a separate registration for professionals, seminars, and channels on Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp and Telegram. What is missing is visibility for the brand and an easier way to buy, and for that a few things need fixing first.

- Search Google for the official BAEHR shop in Spain and you get German shops. When we checked, the AI-generated summary Google showed at the top said it could not find a distributor in Spain.
- Your server blocks bots, including those used by ChatGPT and similar services. Instead of the shop, they get a verification page. If they cannot read the site, they cannot mention it in their answers.
- The catalogue has translation errors and typos such as "FRES FEET" (the range is called "fresh feet"), "Pies seca" and "Serie alovera", plus a category with the URL "uncategorized-es". Anyone searching with the terms the trade uses will not find the product.
- There are three different minimum order values for free delivery: €60 on the site, and €100 and €150 on old pages that Google still shows.
- A single page loads 43 scripts and 44 stylesheets. On a phone, that means seconds of waiting at every step of an order.
- The sign-up form for professionals has 14 fields. We think this is the main thing holding back new sign-ups.

## What we propose for each goal

### Making sure Spain knows who sells BAEHR

To make this clear to Google and AI assistants, we would start here.

- Use the same description everywhere (your site, your Google Business Profile and social media): "Proxy Beauty Spain, S.L., Barcelona, official distributor of BAEHR in Spain".
- Ask the manufacturer to show the same details in its list of distributors. Proxy Beauty Spain is already listed there with baehr.com.es, so it is only a matter of making the details match.
- Let search engines and AI bots in, and describe the company and every product with structured data, a format that Google and those assistants can read.

The quickest gains are in searches like "BAEHR" or "distribuidor BAEHR España", which currently go to other shops. People searching for those already know the brand; they just want to know where to buy it.

### More professional customers

- Pages for each service, such as pedicure or manicure, and for each skin type, written in the words professionals search with: cracked skin, hard skin, sweating, very dry skin. Always presented as cosmetic care, never as treating a condition.
- Seminars as a way in. Each city will have its own page with booking, and afterwards attendees will get an email with access to the portal and a welcome offer.
- A trial kit for each service as a first order. PEDIBAEHR has a 35 ml trial size that fits the idea well.
- Ads for each type of customer: on Google, tied to searches from the trade; on Instagram and Facebook, aimed at beauticians, manicurists and pedicurists.

On timescales, we would rather be clear from the start. For specialist searches (pedicure, podiatry), gaining visibility within three to six months is realistic. For general searches such as "crema de pies", it is not, and we will not promise it.

There is one thing we would rather ask than assume. Looking at your Telegram channel and the seminar held in Russian with Spanish interpreting, our impression is that many of your professional customers speak Russian. Is that right? If so, the idea would be to look after that group through the channels that already work for you, and alongside that, open a new route to professionals who work in Spanish. We would like to talk this through on the call.

### From first click to first order

The site has to work for beauty salons, podiatrists, clinics, manicurists and pedicurists, and for retail customers too. So the home page will have two ways in, one for each.

Instead of the current form, signing up will take two minutes; the prototype shows how. As soon as the account is approved, the salon sees trade prices, and from then on, what matters is that it orders again.

To help with that, each salon will be able to order quickly from a product list, repeat a previous order in one click, and get a reminder when, based on how often it buys, it is probably time to restock. It will also have a calculator showing the cost of each service and the price to charge the client.

## The shop and the portal

The new shop will replace your current site, and we will move the products, orders and customers across. As you mentioned that the manufacturer has no strict requirements on design or structure, the shop will have its own look rather than a template. The catalogue will be in correct Spanish, using the vocabulary of the trade, and the shop will load quickly on a phone. There will be a single minimum order value for free delivery and clear delivery times.

The portal will be the private area for professionals, with everything described in the previous section. Each salon will also find its invoices and order history in its account.
