## What we found

We looked at the site as any visitor would, from the outside, without logging in and without access to your figures.

The foundations are good: BAEHR España has a clear niche, a separate sign-up for professionals, seminars, and channels on Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp and Telegram. Two things hold it back: the brand isn't visible enough, and buying isn't easy enough. A few things need fixing first.

- Search Google for the official BAEHR shop in Spain and you get German shops. When we checked, the AI-generated summary at the top of the results said it couldn't find a distributor in Spain.
- Your server blocks bots, including those used by ChatGPT and similar services. Instead of the shop, they get a verification page. If they can't read the site, they're unlikely to recommend it.
- The catalogue has translation errors and typos such as "FRES FEET" (the range is called "fresh feet"), "Pies seca" and "Serie alovera", plus a category with the URL "uncategorized-es". Professionals who search using the usual trade terms will struggle to find these products.
- Free delivery is offered from three different amounts: €60 on the site, and €100 and €150 on old pages that still appear on Google.
- A single page loads 43 scripts and 44 stylesheets. On a phone, that means waiting several seconds at every step of the checkout.
- The sign-up form for professionals has 14 fields. We think this is the main thing holding back new sign-ups.

## What we propose for each goal

### Making sure Spain knows who sells BAEHR

Here's how we'd make that clear to Google and the AI assistants.

- Use the same description everywhere (your site, your Google Business Profile and your social media), in Spanish, along the lines of "Proxy Beauty Spain, S.L., Barcelona, official distributor of BAEHR in Spain".
- Ask the manufacturer to show the same details in its list of distributors. Proxy Beauty Spain is already listed there with baehr.com.es, so it's only a matter of making the details match.
- Let search engines and AI bots in, and describe the company and every product with structured data, a format that Google and those assistants can read.

The quickest wins are searches like "BAEHR" or "distribuidor BAEHR España", which currently send people to other shops. Anyone searching for those already knows the brand; they just want to know where to buy it.

### More professional customers

- Pages for each service, such as pedicure or manicure, and for each skin concern, using the words professionals actually search for: cracked skin, hard skin, sweaty feet, very dry skin. Always presented as cosmetic care, never as treating a condition.
- Seminars as a way in. Each seminar city gets its own booking page, and afterwards attendees receive an email with portal access and a welcome offer.
- A trial kit for each service as a first order. PEDIBAEHR has a 35 ml trial size that fits the idea well.
- Targeted ads: on Google, for the searches professionals make; on Instagram and Facebook, for beauticians, manicurists and pedicurists.

We'd rather be upfront about timescales. For specialist searches (pedicure, podiatry), gaining visibility within three to six months is realistic. For general searches such as "crema de pies", it isn't, and we won't promise it.

There's one thing we'd rather ask than assume. From your Telegram channel and the seminar held in Russian with a Spanish interpreter, we get the impression that many of your professional customers speak Russian. Is that right? If so, we'd keep looking after that group through the channels that already work for you, and open a new route to Spanish-speaking professionals alongside it. We'd like to talk this through on the call.

### From first click to first order

The site has to work for beauty salons, podiatrists, clinics, manicurists and pedicurists, and for consumers too. So the home page will have two ways in: one for professionals and one for consumers.

We'd replace the current form with a sign-up that takes two minutes. The prototype shows how it works. Once the account is approved, the salon sees trade prices. From then on, the aim is to keep them ordering.

To help, each salon can order quickly from a product list, repeat a previous order in one click and get a reminder when it's probably time to restock, based on how often they buy. The portal will also have a calculator that shows what each service costs the salon and what to charge the client.

## The shop and the portal

The new shop and the portal will both run on Shopify. You'll manage products, orders, customers and prices yourself, from one dashboard. The basics a trade portal needs, such as company accounts, trade price lists, reordering and payment terms, come as standard with Shopify from the Basic plan upwards.

You mentioned that the manufacturer has no strict requirements for design or structure, so we'll build a Shopify theme of its own for BAEHR España instead of using an off-the-shelf template. The catalogue will be in proper Spanish, in the terms professionals use, and the shop will load quickly on phones. Free delivery will start at one amount, the same everywhere, and delivery times will be clear.

We'll move the products, orders and customers across. Passwords can't be carried over to Shopify, so existing customers will get an email to activate their new account. Professionals sign in to the portal with a one-time code sent to their email, so there's no password to remember. The portal will have everything described in the previous section, and salons will also find their invoices and order history in their account.
