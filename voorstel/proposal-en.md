# Proposal for BAEHR España

## More salons and professionals ordering from you

Proposal for Proxy Beauty Spain, S.L. (BAEHR España). November 2026.

You asked us for three things: better brand awareness in Spain, more new professional customers, and more orders from the people who already sign up. This proposal sets out, for each goal, what we would do, what we would build, what it costs and what you can realistically expect.

## Where you are now

What we found from the outside, without logging in and without your own figures.

- Search Google for the official BAEHR shop in Spain and you land on German shops. The AI Overview for those results said no Spanish distributor could be found.
- Your server turns away automated visitors. The crawlers behind ChatGPT and similar services get a verification page instead of your site.
- The catalogue has translation errors: "FRES FEET" (the range is called "fresh feet"), "Pies seca", "Serie alovera", and a category address "uncategorized-es". Professionals search in their own words and then fail to find the product.
- There are three different thresholds for free delivery: €60 on the site, and €100 and €150 on old pages that Google still shows.
- A single page loads 43 scripts and 44 stylesheets. On a phone, that adds seconds to every step of an order.
- The sign-up form for professionals has 14 fields. In our view, that is the biggest single obstacle to winning new professional customers.

What already works: a clear niche, a dedicated sign-up for professionals, seminars, and active channels on Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp and Telegram.

## The plan, goal by goal

### Brand awareness in Spain

- One consistent description everywhere, on your site, your Google Business Profile and social media: Proxy Beauty Spain, S.L., Barcelona, official distributor of BAEHR in Spain.
- Make sure your listing on the manufacturer's partner page carries exactly the same details.
- Let search engines and AI crawlers into your site, and mark up your company and product data so Google and AI services can read it.
- Searches for BAEHR and for "distribuidor BAEHR España" currently go to German shops. That is where the quickest gains are, and we expect to see the first results within three months of launch.

### New professional customers

- Pages for each treatment and skin type, written in the words professionals search with: cracked heels, hard skin, sweaty feet, very dry skin. Always presented as care, never as treating a condition.
- Seminars as a way in: a page for each city with online booking, and after the seminar an email with portal access and an introductory offer.
- A trial set for each treatment as a first order. PEDIBAEHR already comes in a 35 ml trial size.
- Ads targeted by profession: Google for the searches professionals make, and Instagram and Facebook for manicurists, pedicurists and beauticians.
- To be realistic: the professional niche (pedicure, podiatry) is achievable within three to six months. Broad consumer searches such as "crema de pies" are not, and we will not promise them.
- From your Telegram channel and the seminar in Russian with Spanish interpreting, we understand that many of your current professional customers speak Russian. If that is right, we would keep that group engaged through the channels that already work for you, and build a Spanish-speaking pipeline alongside it. We would like to go through this with you on a call.

### From first contact to first order

- Two ways in on the home page: one for professionals, one for retail customers.
- Sign-up in two minutes instead of 14 fields. The concept site shows how this works.
- Trade prices as soon as an account is approved.
- Quick ordering from a product list, reordering in one click, and a reminder when a product is about to run out.
- A calculator for salons: the cost of each treatment and the price to charge the client.
